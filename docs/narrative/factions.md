# Narrative Bible: The Five Factions of Veyr

## 1. Settlement Council
- **Philosophy**: Pragmatic survival for the surface populace.
- **Goal**: Collect embers to power infrastructure (lights, heaters, water filtration).
- **Conflict**: Willing to sacrifice historical records and harvest Ember-Bound souls if it keeps the city warm.

## 2. The Hollow Cult
- **Philosophy**: Nihilistic liberation.
- **Goal**: Prevent the Heart Ember from ever being reassembled.
- **Conflict**: Believes binding memories into a machine is an eternal crime against nature; actively sabotages restoration efforts.

## 3. The Wardens
- **Philosophy**: Strict martial containment.
- **Goal**: Subdue and isolate all ember creatures and rogue machines.
- **Conflict**: Demands total control over weaponized embers; rewards Kael with signature Warden weapon heads.

## 4. The Archivists
- **Philosophy**: Historical truth and intellectual preservation.
- **Goal**: Recover the unvarnished history of Veyr without moral sanitization.
- **Conflict**: Unlocks the Evidence Board deduction system; refuses to destroy record-embers.

## 5. Scavenger Crews
- **Philosophy**: Underground frontier capitalism.
- **Goal**: Excavate and sell rare embers to the highest bidder.
- **Conflict**: Competes with Kael in high-risk deep-shaft extraction races across the Underlevels.

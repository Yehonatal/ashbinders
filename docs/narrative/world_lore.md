# Narrative Bible: The World of Veyr

## 1. Setting & Lore
The ancient city of **Veyr** was built directly atop a massive subterranean magma fissure. The civilization harnessed this heat through the sacred **Heart Ember** — a colossal crystalline flame forged from the condensed memories, emotions, and life force of thousands of ancient citizens.

For centuries, the Heart Ember granted Veyr limitless mechanical power, running water, heated aqueducts, and protection from subterranean monstrosities.

## 2. The Great Shattering
Centuries ago, the Heart Ember ruptured and shattered into millions of ember fragments. The upper city collapsed, plunging into a freezing dark age. 

Without the central flame:
- The subterranean ruins broke into six distinct forgotten regions.
- Trapped human memories coalesced into monstrous **Ember Creatures**.
- Partially transformed survivors became the **Ember-Bound**.

## 3. The Ashbinder Protagonist: Kael
Kael is one of the last living **Ashbinders** — individuals capable of seeing the latent flow of ember energy and physically binding embers with the ancestral **Ashbinder Chain**. Kael descends into the ruins not merely to survive, but to decide the fate of Veyr's past and future.

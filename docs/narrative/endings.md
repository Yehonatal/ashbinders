# Narrative Bible: The Four Endings of Ashbinders

The resolution of Ashbinders is determined by cumulative mechanical choices made throughout the entire campaign (Ember-Bound fates, Ember Capacity harvesting, Faction reputation, and Evidence Board deductions), not by a simple binary final choice.

---

## Ending A — Restoration (The Golden Flame)
- **Condition**: Heart Ember reconstructed; high standing with Settlement Council / Wardens.
- **Narrative**: Veyr's electrical grids ignite, water flows, and the surface city enters a golden age. However, the whispers in the flame fall silent, indicating thousands of souls are once again imprisoned to power the city.

## Ending B — Release (The Natural Dawn)
- **Condition**: Heart Ember permanently shattered; high rate of freeing Ember-Bound NPCs.
- **Narrative**: Remaining ember monstrosities dissolve into peace. The ancient machinery goes dark forever. Humanity must rebuild through its own labor, free from borrowed ancestral memories.

## Ending C — Ascension (The Living Vessel)
- **Condition**: Max Ember Capacity and Ember Resonance; consistent aggressive harvesting of embers.
- **Narrative**: Kael absorbs the Heart Ember directly into their body, becoming an immortal living conduit. The city survives, but is entirely subjugated to Kael's personal will and endurance.

## Ending D — Fusion (The Resonant World)
- **Condition**: Maximum Insight stat, 100% Archivist investigation completed, zero Ember-Bound harvested.
- **Narrative**: Kael uses ancient forge resonance frequencies to weave the trapped memories gently into the living biosphere of Veyr. The world is permanently altered with glowing flora and harmonic energy, achieving peace without sacrifice.

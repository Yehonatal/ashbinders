# Gameplay Specification: The Ember Resource System

## 1. Core Philosophy
"Power is moved, not created."

In Ashbinders, Embers are fragments of the shattered Heart Ember of Veyr, imbued with warmth, kinetic energy, and ancestral human memories. They act as the single unifying currency connecting combat, puzzles, traversal, RPG growth, and philosophical story choices.

---

## 2. Ember Mechanics

### 2.1 Extraction
- When an ember creature is defeated or an ancient machine is drained, an Ember Core crystallizes in the world.
- Kael uses the Ashbinder Chain to extract the Ember via an interactive extraction trigger.

### 2.2 Socketing into the Ashbinder Chain
- Extracted Embers can be socketed into the Ashbinder Chain to modify combat attacks and mobility:
  - **Motion Ember**: Increases move speed by +30% and adds kinetic dash attacks.
  - **Guard Ember**: Adds a precision parry window that absorbs 100% of incoming physical damage.
  - **Forge Ember**: Ignites chain strikes with burning damage over time (DoT).
  - **Bonelight Ember**: Grants lifesteal on hit and illuminates dark zones.
  - **Memory Ember**: Spawns temporal afterimages to confuse enemy targeting.

### 2.3 World Machine Power Routing
- Ancient mechanisms (pumps, elevators, blast doors, power conduits) require specific Ember types to function.
- **The Trade-off**: Placing an Ember into a machine removes it from Kael's combat chain until extracted back.

---

## 3. Ember Capacity & The Ember Tide
- **Capacity**: Kael begins with 1 Ember slot. Completing Vault dungeons rewards Anchor Embers, permanently expanding capacity up to 5 simultaneous Embers.
- **The Ember Tide**: A global dynamic cycle alternating between High Tide (surging power, dangerous creatures, opened secret doors) and Low Tide (dormant machines, safer exploration).

# Gameplay Specification: Deduction & Evidence Systems

## 1. Overview
In Ashbinders, deduction is an active gameplay mechanic rather than passive journal entries. Linked closely with the **Archivists** faction and the mystery of the shattered Heart Ember, players collect clues throughout the world and assemble conclusions on an in-game **Evidence Board**.

---

## 2. Evidence Types
1. **Record-Embers**: Memory fragments containing audio/visual testimonies from ancient inhabitants.
2. **Architectural Murals**: Inscriptions and carvings depicting historical events and engineering schematics.
3. **NPC Testimony**: Contradictory accounts from faction leaders and Ember-Bound survivors.
4. **Physical Artefacts**: Broken machine components, seals, and journal pages.

---

## 3. The Evidence Board Mechanic
- The player opens the Evidence Board in the UI to inspect discovered clues.
- Pins and string connections allow the player to link clues to hypotheses.
- **No Hand-holding**: The game never automatically solves deductions. Reaching a valid conclusion unlocks secret dialogue branches, bypasses hostile encounters, or unlocks the elusive **Fusion Ending**.

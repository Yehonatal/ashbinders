# Ashbinders GDD Reference & World Bible

## 1. Game Overview
- **Pitch**: An open-world dark-fantasy RPG where a lone ember-mage explores the buried, interconnected ruins of the dying subterranean city of Veyr.
- **Protagonist**: Kael, one of the last Ashbinders capable of seeing and routing embers.
- **Core Pillars**:
  1. *Power is moved, not created*: Every system draws from a finite ember resource.
  2. *Continuous open world*: The entire map is explorable from hour one; capability gates progress, not geography.
  3. *Every choice mirrors the ending*: Free embers vs. Harvest embers.
  4. *Unified Ember Logic*: RPG progression, combat sockets, puzzle power, and deduction share the same resource.

---

## 2. The Six Regions of Veyr

1. **The Settlement** (Identity: *What we owe the living*)
   - Living surface/subsurface hub; reacts in real time to ember routing.
2. **The Underlevels** (Identity: *Survival*)
   - Sprawling mining network, Scavenger Crew rivalries, deep shaft dives.
3. **The Drowned Districts** (Identity: *Stability and protection*)
   - Flooded aqueduct zones; map-wide water level manipulation puzzles.
4. **The Bonelight Warrens** (Identity: *What we fear in the dark*)
   - Dark caves; physical layout reshapes in complete darkness; light/dark ember puzzles.
5. **The Sunken Archive** (Identity: *Truth and memory — history itself*)
   - Ancient historical repository; memory-ember time-replay puzzles.
6. **The Furnace Spire** (Identity: *Control over memory and history*)
   - Vertical monolithic spire leading to the Ash Cathedral and Hollow Furnace.

---

## 3. The Five Factions
1. **Settlement Council**: Wants stability; infrastructure restoration quests.
2. **The Hollow Cult**: Believes the Heart Ember should remain broken; sabotage and philosophy.
3. **The Wardens**: Wants strict order and creature containment; Warden weapon heads.
4. **The Archivists**: Wants the true history of Veyr; unlocks evidence board deduction quests.
5. **Scavenger Crews**: Wants profit from rare embers; rival extraction deep-shaft races.

---

## 4. Combat & Ashbinder Chain
- **Primary Weapon**: Modular Ashbinder Chain (chain-whip / kusarigama reach).
- **Modular Heads**:
  - *Blade Head*: Slashing basic attack, balanced speed/damage.
  - *Hammer Head*: High impact, armor breaking, stagger.
  - *Twin Sickles*: Rapid multi-hit attacks, agile repositioning.
  - *Spear Tip*: Precision piercing, extended linear range.
- **Ember Sockets**:
  - *Motion Ember*: Dash-attacks, speed boosts.
  - *Guard Ember*: Parry windows, damage absorption.
  - *Forge Ember*: Fire damage, burning status.
  - *Bonelight Ember*: Lifesteal, dark luminescence.
  - *Memory Ember*: Insight reveals, temporal illusions.

---

## 5. RPG Stats & Deduction
- **Core Stats**:
  - *Vitality*: Max health, damage resilience.
  - *Ember Resonance*: Socket efficiency, Ember capacity power.
  - *Chain Mastery*: Weapon attack speed, combo fluency.
  - *Insight*: Deduction capabilities, dialogue skill checks, secret lore perception.
- **Talent Web**: Non-linear branching web across Combat, Utility, and Reasoning.
- **Deduction System**: Archivist evidence board assembling testimony, murals, and record-embers.

---

## 6. The Four Endings
- **Ending A — Restoration**: Rebuild the Heart Ember; city is saved, but memories are bound to the flame.
- **Ending B — Release**: Destroy the Heart Ember; creatures fade peacefully; humanity rebuilds naturally.
- **Ending C — Ascension**: Kael becomes the living vessel of the Heart Ember.
- **Ending D — Fusion**: High Insight & empathy unlocks releasing memories into the living world itself.

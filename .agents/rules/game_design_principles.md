# Ashbinders Game Design Principles

## 1. The Finite Ember Economy
- Power is moved, not created.
- Every ember assigned to combat is an ember unavailable for puzzle routing, traversal, or world lighting.
- Forcing meaningful trade-offs creates compelling tactical and philosophical gameplay.

## 2. Open World by Capability
- The world of Veyr is physically interconnected and walkable from the opening hours.
- Progression is gated by Ember Capacity, Ember Types, and Weapon Heads — never by artificial invisible walls or arbitrary level numbers.

## 3. Moral Mechanics
- The central dilemma (Free Embers vs. Harvest Embers) must be present in micro-interactions throughout the game (e.g. Ember-Bound encounters, creature fates, machine activations).
- Never present morality as a simplistic "Good vs. Evil" binary; present it as competing needs: survival of the living vs. autonomy of past memory.
